# Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker

**Last updated: June 09, 2026**

Your financial data is yours. This app stores everything locally on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## Data Collection

This app does not collect any data. All expenses, categories, tags, and receipt photos you enter are stored only on your device. No account registration is required and no data is ever sent to any server.

## Permissions

**READ_MEDIA_IMAGES** — Used solely when you choose to attach a receipt photo to an expense. The app does not access your photo library at any other time and does not scan, upload, or analyse your photos.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored in a local database on your device. If you have Google backup enabled, your expense data may be backed up to your personal Google Drive account as part of Android's Auto Backup — this is controlled entirely by your device settings, not this app. Receipt photos are excluded from Auto Backup.

## Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. There are no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or tracking of any kind in this app.

## Data Export

The app provides a voluntary CSV export feature. Any file you choose to export is shared only with the app you select — it is not sent anywhere automatically.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at the same URL with an updated date at the top.
